Context should not disappear

The most useful context is usually the hardest to keep.

Every team has work that lives between systems: what changed, what needs attention, where a handoff is slowing down, and what people are noticing before it becomes a bigger problem.

Keeping up usually means asking people one by one, sitting through another meeting, or piecing together scattered messages. Then a week passes, the context changes, and the whole explanation starts again.

Knavo turns that repeated effort into a short, recurring conversation. The answers stay organized instead of disappearing, giving the team a shared memory without asking anyone to maintain another complicated system.

More than a snapshot

A single check-in tells you what people know now. A series of them starts to show what keeps happening — the team's natural rhythm, recurring friction, and the handoffs that need attention.

At first, Knavo simply brings the right context together. As its understanding deepens, it creates a foundation for smoother handoffs, better routines, and more of the recurring coordination handled for you.

How Knavo works

You set the focus. Knavo talks with the team. The answers come back organized.

01

Anyone sets up a check-in

Tell Knavo what you want to understand, choose the people to involve, and pick a cadence. No technical setup, workflow-building, or manager permission required.

02

Knavo interviews your team

Each person has a short conversation with Knavo on their own schedule. It follows up naturally, helping the important context come through without turning the check-in into extra work.

03

Knavo reports back

The answers come back to whoever set up the check-in, organized around what they wanted to understand. You see what matters now, with earlier context close at hand when it helps.

Clear from the first check-in

Get the context you were looking for.

Each round comes back as a clear, readable view built around the check-in's focus, so you can understand what needs attention without chasing everyone for an update.

Built for continuity

Notice patterns, not just answers.

Because the context carries forward, Knavo can recognize the team's cadence, repeated obstacles, and the small shifts that are easy to miss in a one-time update.

For small teams, not enterprises

Start without a systems project.

No enterprise sales process, admin-seat puzzles, or technical implementation. Choose the focus, the people, and the cadence — Knavo handles the conversation.
